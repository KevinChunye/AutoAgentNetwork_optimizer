Competitive price P* = $2.00, Q* = 6 units, maximum surplus $7.50 per period (Smith 1962, Test 1).

Each turn, one trader who has not traded yet in the period acts: BID or ASK (a quote must improve the book), ACCEPT the standing quote, or PASS. A crossing quote trades at the standing quote's price; both quotes then clear. Buyers cannot pay above their value and sellers cannot sell below their cost. Underlined ids are extramarginal traders (rank 7-11), who should not trade at P*; a trade by one of them lowers efficiency. Efficiency = realized surplus (buyer value - seller cost, summed over trades) / $7.50 per period. Data: results/*/turns.csv on branch market_town of AutoAgentNetwork.
