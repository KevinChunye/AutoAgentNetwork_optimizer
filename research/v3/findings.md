# Findings ledger: Phases 1-3 and the public-data checks (session E5)

One row per finding. Every number was checked against the committed file named in "source". Where Kevin's brief
(session E5, section 3) gave a different number, the ledger uses the file's and says so under "Corrections".

Status, in plain words:
- **confirmatory**: a preregistered test with a Holm family, read once on sealed items.
- **preregistered-descriptive**: a preregistered quantity read once on sealed items, with no pass/fail test.
- **exploratory**: computed on sealed items, but not preregistered as a test, or preregistered as exploratory.
- **post hoc**: chosen after the data it uses were read, or computed on data that were not sealed for it.

Intervals are 95% item-bootstrap intervals unless the source says otherwise. "BBEH 300" means the 300 sealed Phase 1
items of four BBEH tasks (boolean expressions, Dyck languages, geometric shapes, web of lies).

## Corrections to the numbers in Kevin's brief (session E5, section 3)

- **F3.** Sol low costs $0.0062 per call on the 300 sealed items (call 0), not $0.0059. $0.0059 is the mean over all
  of e13_bbeh_open's Sol calls, second calls included. Its per-task accuracy is 92.0-100%, not 91-99%, and its mean
  output is 404 tokens, not 394. Sonnet off costs $0.0139 per call with 1,063 output tokens, not $0.013 and 1,044.
- **F13.** Haiku x5's AUROC is 0.725, not 0.715 (analysis/f13_dilution.py; 0.688 for the mixed 5 matches).
- **F17.** The brief's all-wrong rate of 9.8% and implied 6.3% hold for 38 systems (one without costs left out); on
  all 39 with per-instance details the rates are 8.6% observed and 6.1% under L0 (8.5% under L1).
  [E6 run review: recomputed on the 38 systems with the Atlas code, the observed 9.8% holds but L0 implies 6.6%, not
  6.3% (L1 9.7%, copula 5.9%; research/v4/runs_review_corrections.csv).]
- **F2.** The 12-call panel's n_eff is 3.74 under G2a's estimator (K^2 / sum of the correlation matrix). The
  pruning library's estimator (1' R^-1 1) gives 4.19 on the same calls (information_posthoc.md); both are stated.
