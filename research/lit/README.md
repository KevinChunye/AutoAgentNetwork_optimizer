Nearest threat is 2606.27288 (Chen, 25 Jun 2026): any select-one policy is capped at 1 minus the all-wrong rate, with a Clopper-Pearson certificate, and an LLM router captured none of the gain; its pool excludes pure thinking variants, so depth is still open, but the ceiling is not our novelty.
